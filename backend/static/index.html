About NIRIKSHAN

Sahi Jankari | Behtar Nirnay | Viksit Bharat

NIRIKSHAN is a public-focused digital platform to make information about major Central Sector Infrastructure Projects (₹150 crore & above) more accessible, transparent and easy to understand.

Our Purpose

Our aim is to bridge the gap between infrastructure data and citizens by presenting complex project information in a simple and user-friendly manner. Through NIRIKSHAN, users can explore, track and understand the progress of infrastructure projects across India.

"Informed Citizens. Stronger Infrastructure. A Brighter Tomorrow."

Why NIRIKSHAN?

Infrastructure projects involve large investments and have a direct impact on people's lives. However, project data can often be complex and difficult to understand.

NIRIKSHAN transforms complex infrastructure data into clear, accessible information, enabling citizens to better understand how major projects are progressing across the country.
